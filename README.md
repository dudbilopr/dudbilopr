### Research & Engineering Focus

- **Computational Optics & Digital Holography:** Clean-room numerical wave propagation (Fresnel, convolution Fresnel, evanescent-filtered angular spectrum), zero-order suppression, phase-shifting, CGH encoding (Burch, Lee, Lohmann, Kinoform, Gerchberg–Saxton, OAM fork, DRPE), spatial light modulators (LCoS SLM / DMD), and PyTorch CUDA acceleration (`holom-core`).
- **3D WebGL Digital Twins & Physics Education (`10.Easy`):** Interactive browser-based optical benches and electromagnetic simulators built with Three.js, WebGL, and KaTeX (`HoloLab10.easy`, `Electro10.Easy`).
- **Data Science, Analytics & Educational AI:** Applied machine learning, institutional data analytics, and intelligent academic platforms (`FIMEBOT`, `Snies_Data_Analytics`).
